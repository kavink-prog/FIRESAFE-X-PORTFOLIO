## Claim definitions

- “About 5 minutes” refers to connecting supported devices and the training platform.
- “Within 1 business day” refers to complete organizational setup and onboarding when required access and content are ready.
- Certificate messaging refers to a verified certificate linked to a completed assessment record. It does not claim a numerical accuracy percentage.
- One-click assessment creation is a FireSafeX product capability presented as marketing content; it is not a website assessment-builder feature.
