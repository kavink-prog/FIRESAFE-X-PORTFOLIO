## Prompt used for each source

Use case: identity-preserve. Enhance this real workplace training photograph for a professional fire safety website. Apply subtle exposure correction, neutral white balance, natural contrast, gentle denoising and realistic detail recovery only. Preserve every person's identity, expression, pose, clothing, all equipment and device shapes, screen content, room, and actual activity. Do not add, remove or replace people or objects. Do not invent dashboards, controllers, flames, text or logos. Retain natural skin texture and documentary realism, no CGI, stylization or beauty retouching. Keep the original 4:3 composition and all key subjects visible. Return one enhanced photograph.

These are AI-enhanced real event photographs, not unedited documentary originals. The original files are retained. WebP encoding is used for delivery.
